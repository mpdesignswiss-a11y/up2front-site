Why this page is empty

A review you write yourself is worth nothing

Most sites our size display a rating out of five and a review count from day one. We do not: in Switzerland, a claim of that kind resting on nothing is a misleading statement within the meaning of the Federal Act against Unfair Competition. And incidentally, you would sense it.

What it changes for you

The first client takes a risk. We make it smaller.

You cannot lean on someone else's experience. So we make the risk small: a firm price stated up front, 30 days of unlimited revisions, a domain bought in your name and transferable free of charge, no compulsory subscription. If you leave, you leave with your site.

Six commitments, six articles.

Every line points to an article of our terms and conditions. These are not selling points: they are obligations.

How reviews will get here.

No selection, no sorting, no rewriting. Here is the procedure, written before the first review so that it cannot be adjusted afterwards.

01

On publication

Every client receives, along with delivery, an invitation to leave a review. It is sent to everyone, not just to the projects that went well.

02

On a third-party platform

The review is left on an independent public platform, where we can neither delete nor edit it. The link will point straight there.

03

Published as written

Including mixed or negative reviews. We will be able to reply publicly; we will not hide them.

04

With the real rating

The day an average appears on this site, it will be calculated on the reviews actually left, and the exact number will be shown next to it.

Verifiable right now.
