Who will look after your site in six months?

A site that is not kept up to date ages quickly: a photo to change, a price that moves, holiday opening hours, a security update. Most of our clients have neither the time nor the inclination to deal with it. That is exactly what these plans cover — and nothing is charged to you before your thirty-first day.

The twelve months of hosting, certificate, backups and domain name included in your package come to an end. If you have a care plan, it takes them over automatically: nothing to do, nothing to renew, and not one franc more. Otherwise, renewal goes back to being at your expense.

“A change”, what does that mean exactly?

A change applies to a page that already exists: we replace what is inside it, we do not rebuild the site around it. Better said here than left for you to find out.

  • Included, as often as needed
  • Change a text, a heading, a paragraph
  • Replace a photo or an image
  • Update a price, an opening time, an address
  • Correct or add a link
  • Replace a downloadable document (menu, price list, card)
  • Publish a news item in a section already provided for it
  • Quoted separately, at CHF 90 per hour
  • Create an additional page
  • Add a new section to a page
  • Redo the design, change the visual identity
  • Add a language
  • Add a feature or connect a third-party service
  • Write your texts, take your photos, translate

In other words: a care plan maintains the site you ordered. It does not allow you to obtain a bigger one little by little. If your project grows, we quote for it — and we talk about it beforehand, not after.

And if you take nothing?

Nothing stops: the site stays online and it belongs to you. Changes are simply charged at CHF 90 per hour, with a thirty-minute minimum, and in the thirteenth month you take hosting and the domain back on your own account. A single change a year already costs more than a whole year of care.
