What happens next, step by step.

The production clock does not start now: it starts when your brief is complete. That is the only thing that depends on you.

Step 2

You fill in the brief

One form, about twenty minutes. Your services, your area, your photos if you have any. If you have no copy, we write it from your answers.

Step 3

We produce

The clock runs from the moment the brief is complete, not from payment. Two, three or five business days depending on the package.

Step 4

You review

You receive a review link. Thirty days of unlimited revisions to refine the result.

Step 5

We publish

Domain connected, HTTPS enabled, form tested. The site is live under your own name.

Two things to know right away.

GTC art. 8

30 days of unlimited revisions, or refunded

You will receive a review link. Gather all your comments and send them in one go. Without feedback within thirty days, the site is deemed accepted and published.

GTC art. 6

Check the figures — they are yours

Years in business, number of clients, lead times, certifications: we use what you send us without verifying it. The review period is when that gets checked.

Unsure about what was charged?

Your Stripe receipt lists the package and the options. If anything differs, write to us and we will correct it.
