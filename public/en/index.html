A process of ruthless simplicity

(And how we manage to be this fast and this affordable.)

Step 1

Fill in our brief

About twenty minutes, entirely in writing. Your services, your area, your photos if you have any. The production clock runs from the moment the brief is complete, not from payment.

Step 2

Receive

Production starts as soon as the brief arrives. We study your trade and your area, we set a visual direction, then the page structure and the typography. Three, five or seven business days depending on the package, and you receive a review link.

Step 3

Revise or approve

You have fourteen days to gather your comments and send them in one go: that is the round of revisions included in the price. Once approved, we connect the domain, enable HTTPS, test the form and send you the credentials.

In practice, what do you get out of it — and what does it ask of you?

What you get

  • Fewer calls about opening hours, address and prices
  • Enquiries that arrive at night and at weekends
  • You appear when people search for your trade in your town
  • The client comparing you to a competitor finds you credible
  • One link to send, instead of explaining everything all over again

What it asks of you

  • About 90 minutes in total, spread across the project
  • A brief of a few questions, entirely in writing
  • Your photos — the ones on your phone will do
  • No meetings, no compulsory calls
  • The copy is written for you, from your answers

What we do not promise: a website does not replace word of mouth and will not fill a diary on its own. It makes sure the people looking for you find you, and that the people who find you get in touch.

Frequently asked questions

How can your prices be this low?

No salesperson, no project manager, no meetings: one person, from brief to publication.

The expensive part of a website is not the design: it is everything around it. An agency also bills for its overheads — premises, a sales team, hours of meetings. We removed the overheads, not the work.

Will this website bring me clients?

It makes you findable and credible: that part depends on us, and we do it well. What no honest provider can guarantee is a number of clients — that depends on your trade, your area and your competition. What we do observe is that enquiries arrive mostly at night and at weekends, when you cannot answer the phone.

How much of my time will this take?

About 90 minutes in total, spread across the project and entirely in writing. Your only real task is to gather your photos — the ones on your phone are fine. The copy is written from your answers to the brief: you have nothing to write.

Who is Up2Front for?

For SMEs, sole traders and people starting a business who want a professional website without waiting weeks or paying agency rates. We work across every sector, from local retail to service companies.

Who will design my site?

Maxime Pilloud, who is also the person who replies to you. Up2Front is a sole proprietorship based in Geneva: there is no intermediary, and you are not handed over to anyone along the way.

Do you subcontract to freelancers to keep prices down?

No. The site is produced by the person you are writing to. The price holds because the overheads are kept to a minimum and the process is the same every time, not because the work is subcontracted to the lowest bidder.

Do I own the site?

Yes, once payment has been received in full. The domain name is bought in your name from the outset and transferred to you on request, free of charge, even if you go elsewhere (GTC art. 10).

Will I receive a contract (NDA, assignment of rights)?

We do not provide a standard contract by default, but we are happy to sign an NDA or an assignment of rights that you send us. Once the project is delivered and paid for, ownership of the approved deliverables transfers to you.

What is your design philosophy?

Clarity before effect. A visitor should understand what you do, for whom, and how to reach you, within seconds. Everything else either serves that goal or goes.

How are you different from other agencies?

Our purpose is to make a professional website affordable for every SME, including those without a large budget. The same standard as an agency, at a fraction of the price and the wait.

Which sectors do you serve?

All of them. From the corner shop to the service company, by way of professional practices and startups. Our sites adapt to any activity.

Why do some of your sites look alike? Do you have a niche?

We do not limit ourselves to one sector. If our examples resemble one another, it is the approach that connects them, not a niche: clarity before effect, and as little as possible between the visitor and what they came for. Every project starts from scratch and is adapted to your trade, your audience and your area.

How quickly do you deliver?

Three business days for Landing Pro, five for Site Complet, seven for Site Étendu. These lead times are indicative and run from receipt of a complete brief, not from payment (GTC art. 7).

How do you deliver this fast?

An identical process on every project, a technical base we know inside out, and no meetings. The number of projects run in parallel is deliberately limited: that is what allows the stated lead times to hold.

What if I do not like the site?

One round of revisions is included: you have fourteen days to gather your comments and send them in one go, and we apply them (GTC art. 8). Beyond that, an additional round is charged at CHF 90 per hour. There is, however, no refund once production has started: Swiss law provides no general right of withdrawal for online sales, and we prefer to write that before the order rather than after (GTC art. 5).

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, the amount can be spread over three monthly payments with no interest. The project starts on the first payment.

Can I edit the site myself afterwards?

Yes. We hand over the credentials and a short walkthrough. If you would rather delegate, we handle changes on request.

Do you offer hosting?

Hosting setup is included in all three packages. You remain the owner of your account and your domain name.

Tell me about Up2Front — what is your story?

Up2Front started from a simple observation: in Switzerland, an SME that wants a decent website is caught between a four-figure agency quote and a template it will have to assemble itself. Up2Front is the third way — a professional site, delivered in a few business days, at a price stated up front. The business opened in 2026: you would be among the first clients, and that is written here rather than hidden.
