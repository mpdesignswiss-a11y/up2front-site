Frequently asked questions

Who is this for?

We build websites for entrepreneurs, freelancers, SMEs and self-employed people who want a professional online presence, without waiting weeks or paying big-agency rates.

Who will build my website?

Your website is built by an Up2Front designer and developer. A dedicated contact follows the project to guarantee quality, communication and deadlines.

Do you use AI to build the websites?

The design, the structure and the development are done by humans. AI is not used to “generate a ready-made website”.

Do you outsource to freelancers?

No. Projects are handled in-house, with a single process, to keep a constant level of quality.

Will I own my website?

Yes. You receive ownership of the website, the files and the access details. The domain and the hosting are included in the package.

How fast do you deliver?
  • Landing Pro: 2 days
  • Site Complet: 3 days
  • Site Étendu: 5 days
What if I do not like the result?

You get 30 days of unlimited revisions. If, in a rare case, we cannot satisfy you, a refund is possible.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. All our packages are payable in 3× at no extra cost.

What is your story?

Up2Front is a Swiss agency founded on one simple conviction: every entrepreneur deserves a website worthy of a major brand, without the timelines or the rates of traditional agencies. We deliver websites that are fast, polished and built to convert.
