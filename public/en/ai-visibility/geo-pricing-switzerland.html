How much does GEO cost in Switzerland?

Prices range from zero to several thousand francs depending on what you buy: an automated tool, an audit or ongoing support. Here are the prices Swiss providers publish themselves, with the date we read them.

By Maxime Pilloud, Up2FrontUpdated 27 October 2026

In short

In Switzerland, in October 2026, an automated tracking tool costs CHF 0 to CHF 165 a month, an audit CHF 390 to CHF 4,000, and monthly support CHF 550 to over CHF 1,200 a month. Many agencies publish no price at all. No serious price includes a guaranteed citation.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an AI visibility audit cost in Switzerland?

In October 2026, published audits range from CHF 390 (visiblement.ch) to CHF 2,900–4,000 (Soleil Digital's full audit). Several providers offer a free first test, and Up2Front's Relevé is free.

How much does monthly AI visibility tracking cost?

From CHF 24 a month for a weekly automated tool (CheckAI.ch) to CHF 1,200 a month and more for agency tracking (Soleil Digital, DEEPSEO), according to prices published in October 2026.

Can a provider guarantee a place in ChatGPT?

No. AI answers change from day to day; a guaranteed citation is not a serious promise.
