Cancellation and refunds

Refund policy

Swiss law provides no right of withdrawal online. Here is exactly what that means for you. This English text is a translation; in the event of any discrepancy, the French version prevails.

A reading of the GTC of 9 September 2026

This page creates no right that the general terms and conditions do not provide for. It answers, in the order the questions arise, “can I cancel?” and “can I get a refund?”. Where this page and the general terms and conditions of sale differ, the general terms and conditions prevail.

The principle

Swiss law provides no general right of withdrawal for sales concluded online. Once production has started, the order is non-refundable (art. 5).

Art. 40a of the Swiss Code of Obligations remains reserved for contracts concluded through doorstep selling, as do the mandatory provisions applicable to clients resident in the European Union. If one of those provisions applies to you, it takes precedence over the above.

Before production starts

Production starts on complete receipt of your brief. Between payment and that moment, nothing has yet been produced for you: write to contact@up2front.com and we will look at the request. We do not make a written rule of it because we do not want to turn it into a promise — but no reason pushes us to keep the money for work we have not started.

Past that point, production time is committed and art. 5 applies.

If we cancel

If Up2Front finds itself unable to deliver an ordered project, the sums paid are returned in full. This is not a favour: it is what ordinary law produces when a service paid for is not supplied.

If the site contains an error

The answer is not a refund, it is a correction, and it is included in the price.

  • Before publication — one round of revisions is included, to be requested within fourteen days of the review link being sent (art. 8).
  • After publication — technical errors and typos are corrected for fourteen days, free of charge (art. 12).
  • A change of mind is not an error. An additional round is charged at CHF 90 per hour, subject to a prior quote being accepted (art. 8).

If we stop hearing from you

If your content does not reach us within three months, the project is suspended, your data is kept for twelve months, and payment remains due (art. 11). The production time was reserved; it cannot be recovered.

Within twelve months, a suspended project can resume with no further payment. All you have to do is send us your content.

If you pay in three instalments

All three monthly payments are due. Spreading the payment is a payment facility, not an option to interrupt the order: failure to pay an instalment renders the balance immediately due and permits publication to be suspended until the situation is regularised (art. 4).

The rights in the delivered site transfer to you on payment of the final instalment (art. 9).

What you keep, whatever happens

Your domain name. It is bought in your name, it remains your property at all times, and it is transferred to you on request and free of charge — including if you go elsewhere (art. 10).

How to make a request

Write to contact@up2front.com stating your company name and the order date. We reply within thirty days at the latest, and in practice well before that. An accepted refund is processed by Stripe to the original means of payment; the time to credit then depends on your bank, generally five to ten business days.

A question about this document? Write to contact@up2front.com. We reply within thirty days, and in practice well before that.
