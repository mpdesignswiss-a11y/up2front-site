The visitor's four questions.

We do not start with design. We start with what someone types into their phone before calling you. The site has one job: to answer faster than the competitor next door.

Question 1

What is on the menu, and at what price?

Question 2

Are you open tonight?

Question 3

Where is it?

Question 4

Can I book a table?

The structure delivered.

Here is the actual breakdown, page by page or section by section. This is what the brief fills in, and this is what goes live.

01

Section 1 — the place

One photo, the style of cooking, the town.

02

Section 2 — the menu

A menu readable on a phone, not a PDF you have to pinch to zoom.

03

Section 3 — opening hours

The week set out plainly, closing days included.

04

Section 4 — booking

Clickable phone number and form. If you use a booking tool, we connect it.

05

Section 5 — directions

Address, map, parking, nearest stop.

Why this package rather than another.

A menu changes often. A single page is updated in one place, and loads in under a second on the phone of someone already out on the street.

How a project runs.

Five steps, no compulsory meetings. The clock starts from the completed brief, not from payment.

Step 1

You order

You choose your package and pay in one instalment or three. Payment is handled by Stripe: we have no access to any card data.

Step 2

You fill in the brief

One form, about twenty minutes. Your services, your area, your photos if you have any. If you have no copy, we write it from your answers.

Step 3

We produce

The clock runs from the moment the brief is complete, not from payment. Three, five or seven business days depending on the package.

Step 4

You review

You receive a review link. Fourteen days to gather your comments and send them in one go.

Step 5

We publish

Domain connected, HTTPS enabled, form tested. The site is live under your own name.
