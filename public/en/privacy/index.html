Privacy policy

Version of 28 August 2026. Drawn up in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, for clients established in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This is a translation provided for convenience; only the French version is legally binding.

1. Data controller

Maxime Pilloud, sole proprietorship trading as Up2Front, Rue du Vélodrome, 1205 Geneva, Switzerland. Contact: contact@up2front.com.

2. Data collected

  • Identity data: first name, surname, company name.
  • Contact data: email address, telephone number.
  • Billing data: address, payment information processed by Stripe.
  • Content supplied for the production of the site: text, images, logos, information about your business.
  • Technical browsing data strictly necessary to serve the site.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Performance of the contract — creation, delivery and hosting of the site (basis: performance of the contract).
  • Invoicing and accounting (basis: legal obligation).
  • Communication relating to the project, including reminders (basis: performance of the contract).
  • Responding to your enquiries by email (basis: legitimate interest).

4. Sub-processors and hosting locations

We use the following providers to operate the service. Some are established in the United States, which means the data concerning them is transferred there.

  • Stripe — payments — Ireland and United States.
  • Notion — project management — United States.
  • Tally — forms — Belgium, European Union.
  • Make — automation — European Union.
  • Google Drive — file storage — United States.
  • Vercel and GitHub Pages — hosting — United States and European Union.
  • Resend — sending project emails — United States.

5. Retention periods

  • Accounting records: ten years, in accordance with Swiss law.
  • Project content: twelve months after delivery or suspension of the project.
  • Correspondence: as long as needed to follow up the business relationship.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, object to the processing of, and port your data. To exercise these rights, write to contact@up2front.com. We reply within thirty days.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence in the European Union.

7. Cookies and local storage

This site sets no advertising cookies and uses no analytics tools. The only local storage used remembers the language you have chosen, so that you are not asked again on every visit. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

8. Security

Exchanges with this site are encrypted over HTTPS. Access to the tools used for your projects is protected by two-factor authentication.
