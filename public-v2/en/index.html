The problem

Right now, you get to choose between two bad options.

An agency

Several thousand francs, meetings, weeks of waiting. For a five-page website.

Doing it yourself

An afternoon on an online builder, a result that looks like everyone else's, and an "About" page still empty six months later — because the real obstacle was never the budget, it was knowing what to write.

Up2Front is the third option: a site built for you, at the price and pace of an online builder.

What you get

A finished website, not a building site.

01 A site written for your trade

No copy of your own? You answer a second, shorter questionnaire about your business. We write the pages from your answers, and you read them over before the site goes live. Included in all three packages.

02 A design set up in your colours

Your colours, your photos, your structure, the vocabulary of your trade. The site is assembled around your business rather than poured into a ready-made template.

03 Built for the phone first

Your clients will look you up from their mobile, in the street or on the train. Your site is designed for that screen before any other.

04 The technical groundwork for search

Titles, descriptions, page structure and loading speed: what Google looks at first is done properly, and it is included in the price.

05 Hosting and security certificate included for the first year

The site is live, secured over HTTPS, on your domain name. Nothing to install, nothing to configure.

06 The site belongs to you

No compulsory subscription with us. You can take it to the host of your choice whenever you like. It is your working tool, not our rental.

How it works

Four steps. No meetings.

Step 1 You choose your package

Three packages, three prices on display. Two minutes online.

Step 2 You pay

Card or TWINT, secure payment. The package price is firm: what you see is what you pay, and nothing is added along the way.

Step 3 You fill in a questionnaire

About twenty minutes, whenever suits you, from your phone if you prefer. Don't have your copy yet? A second, shorter questionnaire lets us write it for you — on all three packages, at no extra cost.

Step 4 You receive your site and read it over

We send you a private link. You read it over, you send us your corrections in one go, we apply them, then we publish.

Why there is no phone appointment.

Because a website project doesn't need three meetings — it needs precise information, in writing. A questionnaire collects that better than a call does, you fill it in at your own pace, and that is what lets us show CHF 290 instead of several thousand.

Objections

The questions everybody asks.

"What if I don't like the result?"

You see the site before it is published, on a private link, and you ask for your corrections before it goes live. If the result really doesn't match what you asked for, we talk it through: we don't want an unhappy client with a website online.

"I know nothing about computers."

That is exactly what this is built for. You have nothing to install and nothing to configure: no hosting, no security certificate, no domain connection. You answer a questionnaire in plain language, we handle the technical side.

"Why pay before seeing anything?"

Because that is what replaces the estimate, the chasing and the appointments. Nobody bills you for that time here: it doesn't exist. The price on display is the direct consequence.

"How can you be that fast?"

Because we don't start from scratch on every project. We have built our own tools and our own component library, and everything around the project is automated: the order, the questionnaires, the reminders, the sending. What is left is the work that counts: understanding your trade, writing your pages, designing your site.
