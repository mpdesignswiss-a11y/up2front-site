Prices are stated in Swiss francs. Up2Front is not registered for VAT, so the prices displayed are net prices with no tax to add. The applicable prices are those displayed on the site on the day of the order.

The prices shown on the site are those applicable to Switzerland and Western Europe. For other countries, a price adapted to local purchasing power applies. The exact amount appears on the quotation issued before any order; no adapted price is applied retroactively to an order already paid for.

Outside Switzerland and Western Europe, the full price is due at the time of ordering and the instalment payment provided for in art. 4 is not offered.

4. Ordering and payment

Payment is made at the time of ordering, at the client's choice, either in full or in three equal monthly instalments, interest-free and fee-free, spread over three months. The order is firm as soon as the first instalment is collected and the contract is concluded on receipt of that payment. Payments are processed by Stripe; Up2Front has no access to any card data.

Instalments are not available for orders whose billing address lies outside Switzerland and Western Europe (art. 3bis).

Where payment is made in instalments, rights in the delivered site pass to the client only on payment of the final instalment, in accordance with art. 9. Failure to pay an instalment renders the balance immediately due and entitles Up2Front to suspend publication of the site until the situation has been regularised.

This payment facility falls outside the scope of the Swiss Federal Consumer Credit Act: it is granted free of interest and charges, it is repayable within three months, and it is offered to a professional clientele.

  • Tacit acceptance: without a response from the client within fourteen days of the review link being sent, the site is deemed accepted and may be published.
  • Any further round is charged at CHF 90 per hour, against a quote accepted in advance.
  • Requests that change the nature of the project — an additional page, a feature not provided for — are the subject of a separate quote.

Where the text of the site has been written by Up2Front on the basis of the client's brief (art. 8bis), the round of corrections included in the price covers both layout and wording. The client is required to read the whole of the text during the fourteen-day period, in particular figures, proper names, contact details and service descriptions. The tacit acceptance set out above then also covers the editorial content.

A complete rewrite requested after acceptance, in the absence of an error attributable to Up2Front, constitutes additional work charged at CHF 90 per hour against a quote accepted in advance.
